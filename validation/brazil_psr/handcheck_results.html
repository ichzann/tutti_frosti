Frost damage vs wheat insurance loss

hand-checked wheat fields (southern Brazil, 2019–2021), each matched to one PSR insurance policy. Relative loss = (1 − coverage) + coverage × payout ÷ insured value. "No claim" fields lost less than the 35 % deductible. Each method scores the frost nights from ERA5; a field's value is its most damaged night.

Area match
Keeps only fields whose automatic outline differs from the insured area by at most the chosen hectares (or percent). All 110 fields are within 35 %.
AUC paid vs none
Chance that a random frost-paid field scores higher than a random no-claim field (0.5 = no separation, 1 = perfect; ties count half). Score = damaged share for the methods, the value itself for the baselines.
ρ group
Spearman rank correlation between loss group (none < mild < medium < heavy) and the score.
ρ loss (paid)
Spearman correlation between relative loss and score among frost-paid fields only.
Placebo AUC
The same AUC on a night 35 days before each frost night. It should be near 0.5; a high value means the score separates the groups for reasons other than the frost night.
Baselines
era5_cold = how cold the coldest frost night was; ndmi_drop = field-median NDMI fall within 5–40 days after the night; low_peak_evi2 = how low the season's greenness peak stayed.
