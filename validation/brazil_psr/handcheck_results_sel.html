Frost damage vs wheat insurance loss

automatically selected wheat fields (southern Brazil, 2016–2024), no hand check: outlines kept only if they are one piece without holes, compact (solidity ≥ 0.80), with the policy point near the middle and an area within 15 % of the insured area. Each is matched to one PSR insurance policy. Relative loss = (1 − coverage) + coverage × payout ÷ insured value. "No claim" fields lost less than the 35 % deductible. Each method scores the frost nights from ERA5; a field's value is its most damaged night.

Whole-season detectors: detected damage vs insured loss

No frost night is used. Each detector scans the whole season (emergence to the day before harvest) and keeps every lasting damage event with its date. "Near cold spells" keeps only events whose image interval follows an ERA5 night ≤ 2 °C (up to 5 days before the first image). Area match and year filters above apply here too.

Whole-season detectors
season_drops: per pixel, a drop between two clear images of ≥ 15 % of EVI2, faster than normal ageing (0.017 EVI2 per day = 90th percentile of no-claim development fields after the peak), still at least half there on the next image. frost_recovery_v2: frost_recovery's model, matched filter and calibration, trained and calibrated on the whole season, run with every image date as a possible night and no "already declining" refusal. frost_recovery (frost nights): the original, for reference (share only).
Damage measures
Share = damaged pixels ÷ assessed pixels. Depth = relative EVI2 drop of a damaged pixel (mean over damaged pixels). Share × depth = mean pixel depth over the whole field (undamaged pixels count 0).
Hold-out
30 % of fields, chosen by a fixed hash of the field id (the methods' own lockbox, also left out of frost_recovery's training and calibration). Settings were fixed on the development fields.
Area match
Keeps only fields whose automatic outline differs from the insured area by at most the chosen hectares (or percent). All 188 fields are within 15 % (at most 14.8 ha).
AUC paid vs none
Chance that a random frost-paid field scores higher than a random no-claim field (0.5 = no separation, 1 = perfect; ties count half). Score = damaged share for the methods, the value itself for the baselines.
ρ group
Spearman rank correlation between loss group (none < mild < medium < heavy) and the score.
ρ loss (paid)
Spearman correlation between relative loss and score among frost-paid fields only.
Placebo AUC
The same AUC on a night 35 days before each frost night. It should be near 0.5; a high value means the score separates the groups for reasons other than the frost night.
Baselines
era5_cold = how cold the coldest frost night was; ndmi_drop = field-median NDMI fall within 5–40 days after the night; low_peak_evi2 = how low the season's greenness peak stayed.
